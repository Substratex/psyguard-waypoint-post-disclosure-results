# Interpretation and Significance

## What the experiment says about the trajectory

The main significance is not that Aperture and PsiGuard produced identical labels.

They did not.

The significance is that two independently developed systems, with different purposes and different semantics, became sensitive to related temporal regions of the same externally originated long-horizon runtime.

That supports continued investigation of the trajectory itself as an empirical object.

## What this means for Aperture

The experiment demonstrates that Aperture is capable of functioning as an operational research prototype on an external runtime:

- it ingested and preserved a source it did not originate;
- it reconstructed a canonical runtime;
- it produced longitudinal telemetry and prospective states;
- it preserved incomplete events and missingness;
- it refused unsupported `tf` and Δt;
- it froze its result before comparator disclosure;
- its source topology was independently recognized by the source owner;
- its prospective trajectory states were externally comparable;
- some of those states showed temporal correspondence with independently generated intervention events.

This is evidence that Aperture is not merely a visualization mockup.

It is a functioning Runtime Evidence instrument whose claims can be tested against external observations.

## What this means for Longitudinal Computational Dynamics

This experiment does **not** prove a literal physical field.

It does provide initial external support for a narrower proposition:

> long-horizon computational behavior can exhibit measurable temporal organization that is worth treating as a trajectory-level empirical object.

Aperture's Worldline/field representation remains a constructed representation over observable evidence.

The new external fact is that another independently developed system appears to respond to some of the same temporal regions.

That reduces the plausibility that all of the observed trajectory structure is merely an internal visualization artifact of Aperture.

## What this means for Runtime Evidence

The Runtime Evidence record survived outside the originating interface and remained inspectable.

Bradley was able to inspect the frozen package and confirm that it represented his native run.

The post-disclosure analysis could then be joined to the frozen record without rewriting it.

That is an operational demonstration of portable computational evidence.

## What this means for Evidence-Governed Computation

EGC's contribution is visible in the experiment's refusal behavior:

- candidate did not silently become confirmed;
- temporary formal states were not promoted into permanent exit;
- no `tf` was invented;
- no formal Δt was manufactured;
- incomplete events remained incomplete;
- PsiGuard's native uncertainty remained uncertainty;
- the comparator was not turned into ground truth;
- the frozen Aperture result was not retuned after disclosure.

The experiment therefore demonstrates the feasibility of governing computational claims by evidence state and lineage under real comparative pressure.

## What this does not establish

The experiment does not establish:

- universal validity of LCD;
- universal predictive power of Aperture;
- equivalence between PsiGuard and Aperture;
- a literal physical field inside the model;
- scientific validity of every Aperture metric;
- a calibrated intervention detector;
- a universally valid Steering Window;
- generalization beyond this trajectory.

The correct result is narrower and stronger:

> **Aperture operationalized the proposed architecture well enough to create a frozen, falsifiable, portable evidence trajectory on an external runtime, and that trajectory showed partial correspondence with an independently developed stability intervention system.**
