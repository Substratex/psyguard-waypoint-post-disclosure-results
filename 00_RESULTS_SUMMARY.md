## Core comparison

For prospective interpretation, the primary comparison point is Aperture horizon `T−1`, because PsiGuard's intervention occurs during the answer at T.

| Comparison | PsiGuard intervention turns | Comparable holding turns |
|---|---:|---:|
| Aperture candidate active at prior horizon | **9/11 (81.8%)** | **164/254 (64.6%)** |
| Aperture candidate active at exact horizon | **8/11 (72.7%)** | **165/254 (65.0%)** |
| Within ±1 turn of temporary formal-boundary horizon | **6/11 (54.5%)** | **83/254 (32.7%)** |
| Exact temporary formal-boundary horizon | **1/11 (9.1%)** | **30/254 (11.8%)** |

The candidate state is broad and therefore low-specificity. It is evidence of trajectory concern, not an action-level intervention predictor.

The ±1 formal-boundary comparison is more concentrated around interventions, with an exploratory odds ratio of approximately **2.47**, but the small event count, temporal dependence, and intervention effects prevent confirmatory statistical interpretation.

## Bottom line

The first blind external comparison supports:

> **partial prospective structural and temporal correspondence between independently developed systems operating over the same long-horizon runtime, with substantial semantic and operational non-equivalence.**

It does not establish detector identity, universal prediction, or validation of every Aperture measurement.
